Remote jobs, gathered in one place

JobAgent brings together current remote roles from specialist job boards and company career pages. The public catalog is limited to jobs with evidence that they can be performed from Poland or Bulgaria, so you spend less time opening listings that are not available where you live.

Listings from the last 14 days are normalized into one consistent view. When the same role appears in more than one source, JobAgent merges it into a single listing while preserving its source links. You can filter the catalog using details extracted from the job description, including technology, seniority, engagement type and compensation.

The personal agent and browser automation run on your own computer under your control—not as a hosted bot on this website. Shared job data and your private results are stored by JobAgentWeb, while configured AI providers process the content needed for extraction and ranking. See the technical process and requirements.
