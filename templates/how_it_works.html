From a CV to a ranked shortlist

JobAgentWeb is only the dashboard, everything below happens in JobAgent, running locally on the candidate's own machine, once a day or on demand.

01 · Getting started (once)

A PDF CV becomes a structured profile (stack, seniority, experience). A short questionnaire fills in what a CV can't: salary floor, work mode, dealbreakers. Both live locally and sync into this account's preferences (visible read-only on the Preferences page).

02 · Searching, every day

JobAgent derives its own search terms from the CV and preferences and checks a dozen-plus job boards, LinkedIn for every selected country, worldwide remote boards once as "remote," and Polish boards only if Poland is selected.

03 · Fast, free filtering

Before any AI reads a word: language match, banned/required keywords, and hard rules (pay floor, remote-only) get checked deterministically. Most postings never make it past this stage, for free.

04 · AI scoring

Everything that survives is scored 0–10 against the candidate profile, with a breakdown: stack/seniority/company/pay sub-scores plus concrete pros and cons.

05 · Listwise ranking + second opinion

The strongest candidates are ranked directly against each other (not scored in isolation), then a second, independent model reviews the shortlist specifically for dealbreakers the first pass might have missed.

06 · Learning from decisions

Every apply/reject/reviewed choice made here feeds back into JobAgent: a learned preference profile (shown read-only under "What the agent learned") and calibration examples for cases where the ranking and the actual decision disagreed.

Changing preferences, uploading a new CV, or triggering a run all happen in JobAgent locally, this dashboard is for reviewing results and making apply/reject/reviewed decisions.
